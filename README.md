A GTA 6 meme posted on X: a formal eviction notice from the Leonida Housing Authority, served on one patient gamer after thirteen years of waiting in Los Santos and ordering the move to Vice City. The make.fun ghost appears as the bailiff serving the notice, and the moving costs are paid in full by an AI agent's bounty payout earned on make.fun. The live post is named in POST.json; main.ts copies it to /output for the gate to read.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
